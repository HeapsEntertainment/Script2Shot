# External App Integations Overview
Script2Shot is compatible with multiple apps, such as script writing software, table & spreadsheet editors, text editors, and more, including (but not limited to):
>* Story Architect (STARC)
>* Miscrosoft Excel & Microsoft Word
>* Google Sheets & Google Docs
>* CuePilot
>* Yamdu
>* Windows Notepad
>* Fade In
>* Arc Studio
>* Celtx
>* Trelby
>* WriterDuet
>* Slugline
>* Highland Pro
>* Scrivener
>* Storyist
>* Skrib

# Script Writing Software
## Story Architect (STARC)
Open your STARC project, and select your screenplay file, then
_Right-click>Export_. Under format, select Fountain.
![App Screenshot](assets\screenshot-2026-11-19-213847.png)
Once saved, your screenplay can now be imported into Script2Shot.

## Fade In
Open your Fade IN project. Go to _File>Export_, and select Fountain or Plain Text.
Once saved, your screenplay can now be imported into Script2Shot.

## Arc Studio
Open your Arc Studio project. Select the __Export__ icon, and select _Export File_. Choose Fountain or Text.
Once saved, your screenplay can now be imported into Script2Shot.

## Celtx
Open your Celtx project. Go to _File Menu>Export to Script_. Choose .fountain or .txt.
Once saved, your screenplay can now be imported into Script2Shot.

## Trelby
Open your Trelby project. Go to _File>Export_, and select Fountain or Formatted Text.
Once saved, your screenplay can now be imported into Script2Shot.

## WriterDuet
Open your Trelby project. Go to _File>Export_, and select Fountain or Text.
Once saved, your screenplay can now be imported into Script2Shot.

## Slugline
Open your Trelby project. Go to _File>Export_, and select Fountain (native) or Plain Text.
Once saved, your screenplay can now be imported into Script2Shot.

## Highland Pro
Open your Trelby project. Go to _File>Export_, and select Fountain or Text.
Once saved, your screenplay can now be imported into Script2Shot.

## Scrivener
Open your Trelby project. Go to _File>Export>Files_, and select Fountain or Plain Text.
Once saved, your screenplay can now be imported into Script2Shot.

## Storyist
Open your Trelby project. Go to _File>Export_, and select Fountain or Text.
Once saved, your screenplay can now be imported into Script2Shot.

## Skrib
Open your Trelby project. Select _Export_, and select Plain Text.
Once saved, your screenplay can now be imported into Script2Shot.

# Tables & Spreadsheets
## Microsoft Excel & Google Sheets
### Step 1
Go to _File>Export_, and select _Excel (.xls)_.
### Step 2
Open Excel or Google Sheets. You may receive a warning modal. This is because the file was generated by a html based app. Select "Yes" or "Okay".
### Step 3
If the file is open in a mode such as "Read Only" or "Viewing", be sure to toggle to "Edit Mode".

# Text Editors
## Windows Notepad (Import)
### Step 1
In Notepad, go to _File>Save_, or _File>Save As_.
### Step 2
Save your file as .txt, or .fountain.
### Step 3
In Script2Shot, create a new project, and select _Upload .txt/.fountain_.
### Step 4
In the file picker, select your file saved from Notepad.
### Step 4
Configure your upload settings, and select _Import & Generate Shots_.

## Microsoft Word or Google Docs (Export)
### Step 1
Go to _File>Export_, and select _PDF_.
### Step 2
Open a conversion tool, such as __https://www.freeconvert.com/pdf-to-docx__
### Step 3
Convert the PDF file to .docx format, and save.
### Step 4
Open in Word or Google Docs. You may receive a warning modal. Select "Yes" or "Okay".
### Step 5
If the file is open in a mode such as "Read Only" or "Viewing", be sure to toggle to "Edit Mode".

# External Shotlist Tools supporting CSV
### Step 1
Go to _File>Export_, and select _CSV_.
### Step 2
Open your shotlist tool of choice that supports CSV import. Examples include:
* CuePilot
* Scenechronize
* Yamdu
* Filmustage
### Step 3
Locate the import tool (For example, _File>Import>.csv_).

# Animation Software
